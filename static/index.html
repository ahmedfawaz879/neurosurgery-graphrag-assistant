Neurosurgery-AI GraphRAG Research Assistant

Ask a local question ("what did paper X find?") or a global/synthesis question ("what recurs across this corpus?") about the neurosurgical-AI external-validation literature. Every claim is meant to trace back to a real, cited paper -- verify anything you plan to rely on against the source.

This assistant is a portfolio pipeline demonstration over a 14-paper, one-per-gap convenience sample of real literature -- not a systematic review, and not a substitute for reading the cited papers. See the project README for full Honesty / Limitations notes.
